## What {{appName}} is

{{appName}} is a careers assistant. It helps you describe the skills you have and suggests
where they might be useful. It is not a job guarantee and it does not make decisions about you.

## What we ask of you

- Give honest answers. The profile is only as good as what you put into it.
- Do not use the service to harass anyone or to post unlawful content.
- Do not share an account with someone else.

## What we do not promise

The assistant can be wrong. Check anything important — an entry requirement, a closing date, a
wage — with the organisation that published it.

## Ending your use

You can stop using {{appName}} at any time and ask for your account to be deleted. We can
suspend an account that is being used to harm other people.
