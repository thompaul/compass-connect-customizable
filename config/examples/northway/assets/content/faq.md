## 1. Getting started

### What is Northway?

{{appName}} is a careers assistant run by the Northland Skills Fund. You talk to it about work
you have done — paid, unpaid, at home, anywhere — and it helps you name the skills that work
gave you.

### Does it cost anything?

No. It is free for anyone in Northland.

### Do I need a computer?

No. It works on a phone browser.

## 2. The conversation

### How long does it take?

Most people finish in twenty to forty minutes. You can stop and come back.

### What if I have never had a job?

That is fine, and it is the point. Caring for family, growing food, repairing things, helping at
a place of worship — all of it uses skills, and the assistant is built to find them.

## 3. Your profile

### What do I get at the end?

A skills profile you can download and share with an employer or a training provider.

### Can I change it?

Yes. You can go back and correct anything the assistant recorded.

## 10. If something goes wrong

Before writing to us, try these:

1. Reload the page.
2. Check that you are online.
3. Sign out and sign back in.
4. Try a different browser.

If none of that helps, write to hello@northway.example and say what you were doing when it
stopped working.
