_Example document. Northland Skills Fund and Northway are invented; nothing here is legal advice

**Last updated: 1 October 2026**

## Who is responsible for your data

The Northland Skills Fund operates {{appName}} and decides what personal data it collects and
why. If you have a question about your data, write to hello@northway.example.

## What we collect

- The messages you send to the careers assistant, and its replies.
- The skills and experiences the assistant records from those conversations.
- Your email address, so you can sign back in.

## Why we collect it

To produce your skills profile and to improve the assistant. We do not sell your data and we do
not use it to advertise to you.

## How long we keep it

Until you ask us to delete it, or for two years after your last sign-in, whichever comes first.

## Your choices

You can ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to delete it, by writing to
hello@northway.example.
